# Injury audio

Sources supplied locally by the project owner.

- heartbeat.mp3: Тихий звук биения сердца.mp3, 0.9–10.1 seconds. Low-frequency pulse; loop cut in the quiet intervals and faded at both edges.
- crawl.mp3: Sonniss GDC 2026, Epic Stock Media Fantasy Game 2, CLOTHFlp Action Inventory Open Flip Cloth Canvas Bag Slide Light 02. Cloth movement adapted to crawling with high-pass/low-pass filtering and a modest slowdown.
- stim-start.mp3: Call of Duty Warzone — Player Restored, 0–1.4 seconds. Restoration cue; the original has about1.7 seconds of trailing silence.
- stim-complete.mp3: Call of Duty Warzone — Player Revived, 0–2.5 seconds. Recovery completion cue with the trailing silence removed.

Selection used file labels, duration, amplitude measurements and logarithmic spectrograms. No claim of listening verification. Runtime gains are intentionally moderate to retain audible navigation and speech.
